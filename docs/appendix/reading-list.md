# Reading and Watch List

The books, newsletters, podcasts and talks this coursebook draws on. Links go to official publisher, author or creator pages.

## Core books

| Book | Author(s) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Inspired](https://svpg.com/books/product-is-hard-svpg-box-set) (2nd ed. 2017) | Marty Cagan | How strong product teams discover and deliver products |
| [Empowered](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/empowered/9781119691297/) (2020) | Marty Cagan & Chris Jones | Building and leading empowered product teams |
| [Transformed](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/transformed/9781119697336/) (2024) | Marty Cagan & SVPG partners | Moving a company to the product operating model |
| [Escaping the Build Trap](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/escaping-the-build/9781491973783/) (2018) | Melissa Perri | Moving from shipping features to delivering outcomes |
| [Product Roadmaps Relaunched](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/product-roadmaps-relaunched/9781491971710/) (2017) | C. Todd Lombardo, Bruce McCarthy, Evan Ryan & Michael Connors | Outcome-based roadmaps and prioritisation |
| [Lean Analytics](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/lean-analytics/9781098168179/) (2013; O'Reilly lists a 2024 release) | Alistair Croll & Benjamin Yoskovitz | Choosing the right metric for your stage |
| [Good Strategy Bad Strategy](https://penguinrandomhouse.com/books/208668/good-strategy-bad-strategy-by-richard-rumelt/) (2011) | Richard Rumelt | Telling real strategy from fluff |
| [User Story Mapping](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/user-story-mapping/9781491904893/) (2014) | Jeff Patton | Planning work around the user's journey |
| [Decode and Conquer](https://www.goodreads.com/work/editions/40168005-decode-and-conquer-answers-to-product-management-interviews) (5th ed. 2025) | Lewis C. Lin | PM interview frameworks (CIRCLES and more) |
| [Cracking the PM Interview](https://www.crackingthepmcareer.com/authors) (2013) | Gayle Laakmann McDowell & Jackie Bavaro | PM careers and interviews; the same authors also wrote *Cracking the PM Career* |

More books, newsletters, podcasts and talks are added here as each chapter is written.

## Added with section A8 Product Metrics

- [Amplitude: The North Star Playbook](https://amplitude.com/books/north-star): a free guide to choosing a North Star metric and its inputs.
- [Google Research: Measuring the User Experience on a Large Scale (HEART)](https://research.google/pubs/measuring-the-user-experience-on-a-large-scale-user-centered-metrics-for-web-applications/): the original 2010 paper behind the HEART framework.
- [Dave McClure: Startup Metrics for Pirates (AARRR)](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/startup-metrics-for-pirates-presentation/629833): the 2007 talk that introduced pirate metrics.
- [Lenny Rachitsky: What is good retention?](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-good-retention-issue-29): good and great retention benchmarks by product type.
- [Jorge Mazal: How Duolingo reignited user growth](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth): a real growth model and North Star story.
- [a16z: Retention Is All You Need](https://a16z.com/ai-retention-benchmarks/): retention for AI products (2025).
- [RevenueCat: State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/): subscription and AI app benchmarks.
- [Lenny's Podcast (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@lennyspodcast): long-form interviews with product leaders.

## Added with A0.1 What a PM actually does

- [Martin Eriksson: What, exactly, is a Product Manager?](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/what-exactly-is-a-product-manager/): the 2011 post behind the business–UX–technology picture of the role.
- [Ben Horowitz: Good Product Manager/Bad Product Manager](https://a16z.com/good-product-manager-bad-product-manager/): a classic short essay on the habits of strong PMs.
- [SVPG: Product risk taxonomies](https://svpg.com/product-risk-taxonomies): Marty Cagan on the four big risks.
- [Lenny's Podcast: Tomer Cohen on LinkedIn's full stack builders](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/why-linkedin-is-replacing-pms) (Dec 2025): how AI is reshaping PM roles.

## Added with B0.1 What makes AI products different

- [Microsoft: Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/) (CHI 2019) and the [HAX Toolkit](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ai-guidelines/): 18 design guidelines for AI products.
- [Google People + AI Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/chapters): free chapters on designing human-centred AI.
- [Co-Intelligence](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/741805/co-intelligence-by-ethan-mollick/9780593716717/) (2024), Ethan Mollick: a non-technical guide to working with AI.
- [AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) (2025), Chip Huyen: building products on foundation models, including evaluation and cost.
- [Stanford HAI AI Index](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report): the yearly reference on AI capabilities, cost and adoption.

## Added with A0.2 Product teams

- [SVPG: Product vs. Feature Teams](https://svpg.com/product-vs-feature-teams) (2019): Marty Cagan's short essay on delivery, feature and empowered teams.
- [Teresa Torres: Product Trios](https://www.producttalk.org/product-trios/): what a trio is and how to start one.
- [Lenny's Newsletter: In defense of feature team product](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/in-defense-of-feature-team-product): a counterpoint to the empowered-team ideal.
- [AWS: Amazon's two-pizza teams](https://aws.amazon.com/executive-insights/content/amazon-two-pizza-team/): small teams with single-threaded ownership.

## Added with B0.2 How LLMs work, for PMs

- [Andrej Karpathy: Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI) (2025): a general-audience video on how LLMs are built.
- [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) (2017): the transformer paper.
- [Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback](https://huggingface.co/papers/2203.02155) (2022): the InstructGPT/RLHF paper.
- [OpenAI: Why language models hallucinate](https://openai.com/index/why-language-models-hallucinate/) (2025).
- [Anthropic: Tracing the thoughts of a large language model](https://www.anthropic.com/research/tracing-thoughts-language-model) (2025).

## Added with B0.3 Choosing models

- [OpenAI: Model selection](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/model-selection): set acceptance criteria, test on real tasks, keep the lightest setting that passes.
- [Artificial Analysis: performance benchmarking methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/performance-benchmarking): plain definitions of time to first token, output speed and end-to-end time.
- [LMSYS: RouteLLM](https://lmsys.org/blog/2024-07-01-routellm/) (2024): evidence on cost savings from routing between strong and weak models.
- [Epoch AI: LLM inference price trends](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/ai-inference-price-trends) (2025): how fast the price of each capability level has fallen.

## Added with A0.3 Product vs project vs program management

- [SVPG: Product Management vs. Project Management](https://svpg.com/product-management-vs-project-management) (2007): Marty Cagan's case for separating the roles.
- [The Scrum Guide](https://www.scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html) (2020): the official definition of the Product Owner accountability.
- [PMI: Program and project management: relations, commonalities, differences](https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/program-project-management-relations-commonalities-differences-10591) (2013).
- [Mind the Product: Why you need to know about different product manager types](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/why-you-need-to-know-about-different-product-manager-types) (2021).
